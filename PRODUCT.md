# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Freelancers, finance managers, small business owners, and agency operators who need to issue, track, and reconcile multi-currency invoices, tax withholdings (TDS/withholding tax), overdue aging, and cash flow receivables without the complexity or spreadsheet fragility of Excel.

## Product Purpose

Invoice Tracker is a precision financial receivables ledger that replaces manual Excel sheets (`Invoice Tracker.xlsx`) with automated aging calculations, multi-currency conversion, one-click tax deduction reconciliation (e.g. 15% withholding), client tracking, and professional PDF invoice generation.

## Brand Personality

- **Precise**: Strict mathematical and tabular accuracy with monospace numerals and clear net vs gross calculations.
- **Restrained**: Stripe/Linear-grade product UI with subtle elevations, quiet borders, and semantic status indicators.
- **Frictionless**: Zero setup, instant offline persistence, 1-click Excel import/export compatibility.

## Anti-references

- SaaS AI slop (oversized screaming numbers, random gradient text, decorative glassmorphism blurs).
- Clunky enterprise ERP forms with nested modals, confusing dropdowns, and slow interactions.
- Generic beige/sand paper themes that reduce contrast and readability.

## Design Principles

1. **Earned Familiarity**: Standard, intuitive controls (data table, search, filters, slide-overs) that disappear into the user's workflow.
2. **Tabular Density & Precision**: Tabular numerals, right-aligned monetary values, clear currency badges, and explicit net/gross breakdowns.
3. **Actionable State**: Clear semantic visual cues for Received (emerald), Pending (amber), and Overdue (rose) with immediate settlement actions.
4. **Complete Interoperability**: Flawless bidirectional sync with `.xlsx` spreadsheets ensuring zero data lock-in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large/bold text).
- Keyboard accessible navigation, Esc to close dialogs, focus rings on all interactive elements.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support for all transitions.
